# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sunshine Travel serves passengers, drivers, and operators. The passenger miniapp is used on mobile during booking, payment, trip review, wallet checks, coupons, invoices, and support flows.

## Product Purpose

The product helps users complete local travel tasks quickly and confidently: request rides, track trips, pay fares, manage benefits, and review personal account activity.

## Brand Personality

Warm, practical, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative tech styling, dark dashboard aesthetics, exaggerated gradients, oversized marketing heroes, and generic AI-looking card stacks. Passenger surfaces should feel like a mature daily-use mobility app.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the user's current task obvious.
2. Use orange as a restrained Sunshine accent, not as decoration everywhere.
3. Make money, status, route, and time easy to scan at a glance.
4. Prefer stable familiar mobile patterns over novelty.
5. Keep motion short and purposeful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Prioritize readable contrast, stable tap targets, concise Chinese UI text, color plus text for payment states, and reduced visual noise for quick outdoor mobile use.
