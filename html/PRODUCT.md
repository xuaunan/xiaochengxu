# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Passengers, drivers, and operators use the Web portal as a Sunshine Travel access point for ride booking, dispatch visibility, orders, coupons, and account entry points.

## Product Purpose

Sunshine Travel Web Portal connects the passenger and driver experiences with the existing business APIs. Success means the first screen immediately feels like an active travel-dispatch product, not a static brochure.

## Brand Personality

Bright, trustworthy, operational.

## Anti-references

Do not copy the miniapp layout. Do not make the portal a generic marketing page, an orange-only template, or a stack of decorative cards. Do not blur operational text with visual effects.

## Design Principles

- Show the dispatch system in motion.
- Keep the interface warm and readable.
- Make animated details feel purposeful, lightweight, and product-native.
- Preserve the Web portal scope under `D:\xiaochengxu\html`.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, responsive text, and reduced-motion fallbacks for loop-heavy animation.
